Delete your account

Kernel Panic (com.aceious.elzero) by aceious

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Kernel Panic and go to the board tab.
  2. In the account panel, tap delete account.
  3. Confirm. If the app asks, sign out and sign in again first, then repeat.

Option 2: by email

Email jinamsancheti2@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Kernel Panic account". We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address, profile picture link and user ID
  • Your saved progress: points, streak, finished lessons, flagged questions, review cards, trophies and notes

Nothing is kept afterwards. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you never signed in, there is no account: your progress lives only on your device and goes away when you reset the app or uninstall it.
