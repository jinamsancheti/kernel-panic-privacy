Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · App: Kernel Panic (com.aceious.elzero) · Developer: aceious

  • No tracking
  • No data sold
  • Sign-in is optional

The short version. Kernel Panic teaches the Arm A-profile architecture in short lessons. You can use the whole app without an account, and then nothing about you leaves your device. If you choose to sign in with Google, we store your name, email and learning progress so it follows you to other devices. You can delete all of it at any time.

What we collect

If you do not sign in

Nothing is collected. Your progress (points, streak, finished lessons, flagged questions, notes, review cards and trophies) is saved only on your device and is removed when you reset the app, clear its data or uninstall it.

If you sign in with Google

DataWhy
Name, email address, profile picture link and a user ID, provided by your Google account To create your account and show who is signed in
Learning progress: points, streak, finished lessons, flagged questions, review cards, trophies, and any notes you type in the app To back up your progress and restore it on any device
The time your progress was last saved To keep devices in step
Technical data such as IP address and device/browser details, logged automatically by the sign-in service Security and abuse prevention

What we never collect

No location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera access, analytics or payment details.

Read aloud

The read-aloud button hands the lesson text to the text-to-speech engine already on your device. The app does not record audio. How that engine handles the text is covered by its own provider's policy.

Where your data is kept and who handles it

Account and progress data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which acts as our service provider. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and each account can read and write only its own progress. Google may process data on servers outside your country. See Firebase's privacy information and Google's privacy policy.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and progress until you delete your account. Signing out removes the progress from that device but keeps it in your account.

Your choices

  • Use the app without an account. Sign-in is optional.
  • Delete your account and data. In the app, or by email. See how to delete your account.
  • Ask for a copy or a correction of your data by emailing us.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Children

Kernel Panic is made for adult learners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has signed in, email us and we will delete the account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
